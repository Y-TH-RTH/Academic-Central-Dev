Launch a student tutoring program at your school.

Academic Central is a toolkit for student-run tutoring programs. Recruit tutors, run every session, count volunteer hours, and keep families in the loop, all in one place.

How to start a tutoring program

  1. Find a sponsor. Ask a teacher or administrator to back the program and approve a room and a time.
  2. Define the need. Decide which subjects, grade levels, days, and rooms you will cover.
  3. Recruit and vet tutors. Collect applications with grades and teacher recommendations, then approve your team.
  4. Run your sessions. Tutors sign students in each day, and the program tracks attendance and hours for you.
  5. Show your impact. Share attendance with families and report volunteer hours and progress to your school.

How Academic Central helps

  • Applications: students apply with subject grades and teacher recommendations, and admins review them.
  • Shared sign-in sheet: each room gets one sheet that several computers can use at once.
  • Automatic hours and attendance: tutors get their volunteer hour, and a student counts as present after 50 minutes.
  • Parent portal: families see a weekly attendance grid for their student.
  • Absence requests: tutors ask for time off and the program president approves or denies it.
  • Roles: Head Admins, admins, tutors, and parents each see only what they need.
